> **Stage:** Seed

## Why no placement agents or investment banks

Seed-stage raises sit well below placement-agent economic threshold (~$10M deal size). Their 5-7% fee can't be earned on $2M-$4M, so they won't engage. This is correct for stage, not a gap to fill. Use the five-tier channel approach below.

## Capital strategy: five-tier approach

### Tier 1: Non-dilutive (largest leverage given RedZone's federal traction)

For a defense / field-medicine play with existing federal interest, grants can substitute for substantial equity capital. This should be the primary channel, not a sidecar.

- **SBIR Phase II** ($1M-$2M) — apply if not already in pipeline
- **ARPA-H program awards** ($5M-$50M) — existing relationship; current solicitations tracked monthly
- **BARDA** — biodefense / pandemic-preparedness framing applies for field medicine
- **DoD CDMRP** (Congressionally Directed Medical Research Programs) — combat casualty care, trauma medicine
- **DoD AFWERX, SOFWERX, NavalX** — innovation arms with discretionary funding authority
- **Defense Innovation Unit (DIU)** — accelerated contracting for defense tech
- **NIH grants** for any indication-specific angle that applies

### Tier 2: Defense-tech VCs (primary equity channel)
