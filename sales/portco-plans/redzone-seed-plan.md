# RedZone — Seed Capital Plan

> **Stage:** Seed (active, market-push mode)
> **Target raise size:** $1.5M-$4M (verify against current internal numbers)
> **Close mechanism:** AngelList syndicate (10XBeta) with RedZone seed as SPV underneath. 506(b) private configuration, self-certification (known-LP network). Single close then wire. Multiple-close availability to be confirmed directly with AngelList.
> **Existing federal traction:** DoD SOCOM backing, ARPA-H program engagement (continues producing runway, not the path to close).
> **Strategic acquirers (long-term):** DoD primes (Lockheed, RTX, General Dynamics, L3Harris, Northrop Grumman, BAE), emergency-medicine OEMs.

## Why no placement agents or investment banks

Seed-stage raises sit below placement-agent economic threshold (~$10M deal size). Their fee can't be earned at $2M-$4M, so they won't engage. Correct exclusion, not a gap to fill.

## Strategic positioning of grants

RedZone has real federal traction (ARPA-H, SOCOM). Those existing relationships continue producing runway. **But new grant applications take 6-12 months and don't fit the seed-close timeline.** Treat grants as runway extension, not as primary capital strategy. The seed round needs to close on equity from VCs + strategic CVCs + angels, with grants as background.

## Existing grants: continue, but don't time the round to them

Where already in motion, continue maxing federal channels:
