# Validose — Seed Capital Plan

> **Stage:** Seed (active, market-push mode)
> **Target raise size:** $1.5M-$4M (verify against current internal numbers)
> **Lead indication:** Drug adherence / drug delivery (confirm specific clinical indication — drives which strategics and foundations apply)
> **Close mechanism:** SPV (likely under the 10XBeta AngelList syndicate or law-firm structured). 506(b) private, known-LP network.
> **Strategic acquirers (long-term):** Pharma cos, BD, Insulet, Tandem Diabetes Care, drug-delivery device makers.

## Why no placement agents or investment banks

Same stage logic as RedZone. Seed-stage raises sit below placement-agent economic threshold. Correct exclusion, not a gap.

## Strategic positioning of grants

For Validose specifically, there's likely no in-flight federal grant the way RedZone has DoD/ARPA-H traction. **Don't apply for grants as primary capital path** — application cycles are 6-12 months and won't help the seed close timeline. NIH SBIR Phase I can be filed in parallel as runway extension if the indication fits, but don't time the round to it.

## Capital strategy: strategics-first, four-tier approach

### Tier 1: Pharma corporate VCs (strategics at seed — highest fit)

Drug delivery sits directly adjacent to pharma's core business. Pharma CVCs have explicit thesis fit, write seed checks, and have strategic motivation to invest (drug-delivery innovation extends their molecule franchises). These are **strategics that act as seed investors** — the relationship-building outcome of Track 1c collapses into the actual round.

- **Pfizer Ventures** — active in early-stage drug delivery
- **Eli Lilly Ventures** — does seed for delivery platforms
- **GSK Ventures**
- **Novartis Venture Fund**
- **Sanofi Ventures**
- **AbbVie Ventures**
- **Merck Global Health Innovation Fund**
- **Bayer Leaps** — often early, can write larger checks
- **AstraZeneca Innovation**
- **Boehringer Ingelheim Venture Fund**

Likely Validose's single highest-fit investor category. Direct outreach + warm intros via pharma operator network.

### Tier 2: Strategic medtech CVCs (more strategics at seed)

- **BD Strategic Investments** — highest fit; BD is a leading drug-delivery device company. Worth a dedicated relationship path.
- **J&J Innovation** — broad medtech fit, writes seed checks
- **Olympus Innovation Fund**
- **Insulet** — corporate development, watches the drug-delivery space
- **Tandem Diabetes Care** — corporate development (if diabetes is the lead indication)

Tier 1 + Tier 2 together cover the strategics-at-seed thesis: Validose lands its strategic relationships at the seed round, not 18 months later.
