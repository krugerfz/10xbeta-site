Lock This Week

Six decisions.

  1. RedZone target raise size. Lock at $1.5M, $2M, $3M, or $4M. Drives lead-investor profile and syndicate sizing.
  2. Validose target raise size. Same lock. Drives whether pharma CVCs co-invest or one CVC leads outright.
  3. Validose lead clinical indication confirmed. Drives which pharma CVCs are highest-fit (diabetes pulls Lilly + Novo, oncology pulls Pfizer + Merck, etc.).
  4. RedZone lead-investor priority list (top 3). Out of the defense-tech VC list, pick the three to lead with for first-conversation outreach.
  5. Validose lead-investor priority list (top 3). Same, from the pharma CVC list. BD Strategic Investments runs parallel as strategic anchor.
  6. AngelList multiple-close mechanics confirmed. Direct conversation with AngelList. Affects whether we run a single close or staged closes for RedZone SPV.
