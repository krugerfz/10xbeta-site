The Mental Model

Lab work creates portcos. Capital work matures them.

The studio model needs both. Discovery without funding is interesting science. Funding without discovery is a fund, not a studio. We do both because each compounds the other.

What lab work produces

  • New gaps identified through Opportunity Engine
  • POCs built in weeks, not months
  • Provisional patents filed
  • New portcos spun out, founder shares for studio
  • The pipeline that fills 12-24 months from now

What capital work produces

  • Active portcos reach clinical-grade prototypes
  • Regulatory pathways get resourced
  • Teams scale beyond founding
  • Studio's existing equity matures toward exit
  • Validation that the model works

Without lab work, the studio dries up over time. Without capital work, the studio's portfolio stalls. Neither is optional and neither is "the main thing". Both equally weighted.
