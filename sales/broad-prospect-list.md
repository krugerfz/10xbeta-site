# Broad Prospect List — Lab Revenue Outreach

> **Purpose:** A wide, named pool of potential lab clients to reach out to. Companies that plausibly need outsourced device/hardware/software/regulatory/manufacturing development — the work the 10XBeta lab sells. Spans medtech, defense/dual-use, pharma/delivery, dental, vet, consumer health, diagnostics, and any regulated-hardware adjacency.
>
> **Companion to:** `sales/strategics-target-list.md` (the eight big strategics + outsourcing segments), `sales/lab-revenue-3-week-sprint.md`, `sales/lab-revenue-pipeline.md`.
>
> **How to use this:** This is a sourcing pool, not a vetted call list. Each name is a starting point — look it up, confirm current stage/need, find the warm path if one exists, then promote to the pipeline tracker. Names are grounded in 2025-2026 funding/activity but verify before outreach.
>
> **Fit filter (apply to every name):** Best fits are companies with (a) a real hardware/device/regulated product, (b) no large in-house engineering bench OR a capacity/specialty gap, and (c) a fundable inflection ahead (next round, FDA milestone, POC needed). The giants with deep internal teams (Anduril, Shield AI scale) are poor direct clients — pursue their suppliers and smaller peers instead.
>
> **Last updated:** 2026-06-08

## SEGMENT 10 — Established device makers (overflow / specialty gap)

Mid-size device companies whose teams are at capacity or lack a specialty (optics, embedded, AI, firmware). Offshore cost advantage (SA + Poland) is the differentiator. S4/S5 scope.

- Mid-cap and private device manufacturers below the big-eight tier.
- **Where to mine:** MD&M / MD&DI exhibitor lists; MedTech 100 mid-tier; FDA 510(k) recent-clearance filings (signals an active dev program).

## Sourcing playbook (turn this into names fast)

1. **Open the trackers** — the lists cited above (YC, MedTech Innovator, Value Add VC defense, Crunchbase, DoD SBIR) are pre-filtered pools. Pull 10-20 names each.
2. **Apply the fit filter** — real product, no big bench, fundable inflection ahead.
3. **Find the warm path** — cross-reference every name against the studio network and the named VC portfolios. A warm intro beats cold 10:1 in the sprint window.
4. **Promote to pipeline** — each name worth contacting gets a row in `lab-revenue-pipeline.md` with channel code and sprint offering.
5. **Sequence by warmth, then by hook** — warm + matching portfolio hook (Spiro→respiratory, Validose→delivery) first.
