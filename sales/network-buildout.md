# 10XBeta Investor & Buyer Network Buildout

> **Purpose:** Specific firms, individuals, and external resources to help 10XBeta build its named investor and buyer network across stages. Companion to `portco-capital-playbook.md` (what the strategy is) and `capital-strategy.html` (how it's presented).

> **Target state:** 50-80 named investor relationships across stages, 5-10 secondary buyer relationships, 8-15 strategic CVC contacts. Each relationship has a named contact, a current status (cold / warm / active), and a path to engagement.

> **Verification status:** Firm and individual names below are from training-data knowledge. Many will need verification before outreach. Some may have moved firms, retired, or shifted focus. Treat as a starting list, not a verified contact sheet.

---

## The Five Leverage Sources

### 1. Internal warm sources (free, highest quality, slow to scale)

Relationships 10XBeta already has, ranked by value:

- **Existing portco CEOs.** Each portco has raised from someone. Each CEO knows 5-20 active investors. Map these systematically.
- **Existing studio and portco investor base.** Whoever has invested to date — they have networks.
- **Personal networks across the team.** Brooklyn Navy Yard, Anduril, Tesla, federal contracting, prior medtech employer alumni networks.
- **Existing strategic relationships.** ARPA-H program officers, DoD contacts (RedZone, PARADIGM). These connect outward to other strategics.

**First action:** build `portco-investor-map.md` listing every existing portco investor with current contact status. Likely surfaces 30-60 names already in the network.

### 2. Paid external help (fast scale, costs equity or cash)

Four categories worth knowing:

**A. Fractional Head of Fundraising / Capital Strategy**
- Profile: ex-VP at a healthcare investment bank (Leerink, HC Wainwright, Piper Sandler), or ex-Partner at a medtech VC
- Cost: $8K-$15K per month for 1-2 days per week
- Value: instant access to 100+ investor relationships, deal coaching, term-sheet negotiation
- Where to find: LinkedIn search for "ex-Leerink advisor", "former Versant partner consultant", "ex-Polaris medtech advisor". Healthcare-focused executive search firms (Heidrick & Struggles, Spencer Stuart, Russell Reynolds) can identify candidates.
- Vetting risk: claimed network vs actual network. Reference-check with 3+ founders they've worked with.

**B. Outsourced fundraising agency**
- More structured, lower cost, less senior network access
- Cost: $5K-$25K for a 3-6 month engagement, sometimes with equity component
- Value: outreach engine, CRM management, scheduling capacity
- Risk: can look "shopping" to investors if poorly executed. Quality varies widely. Generally less leverage than (A).

**C. Advisory board with equity grants**
- Ex-strategic execs (already in Session 004 plan) + ex-medtech bankers + ex-medtech VC partners
- Cost: 0.25%-1.0% equity per advisor over 2-4 year vest
- Value: warm intros at strategic credibility level, cross-stage network
- Target composition: 2 ex-strategic execs (Medtronic, J&J, Stryker, BSC tier) + 1 ex-medtech VC + 1 ex-healthcare banker

**D. Placement agent on early retainer**
- Some boutiques will engage on a Series A retainer before the round is live
- Outcome Capital positions for this kind of relationship
- Cost: $5K-$25K retainer + 5-7% success fee on raised capital
- Value: entire deal book opens, full process partnership
- When to engage: when first portco is 6-12 months from Series A readiness

### 3. Industry events and accelerators (mid-cost, structured network access)

**MedTech Innovator** — the premier medtech accelerator. Alumni network of 500+ companies. Their investor day connects companies with 100+ medtech investors. Worth applying portcos even if 10XBeta doesn't need acceleration itself.

**JPMorgan Healthcare Conference (January)** — single highest-leverage annual venue. Already in Session 004 plan. 15-20 strategic meetings possible in 4 days. Side events and dinner rooms are where networking actually happens.

**AdvaMed** — major medtech industry association. Annual conference brings together strategics, investors, and operators. Membership has investor relations value (~$5K-$15K per year by tier).

**MedTech Strategist** — publication that runs investor-meets-startup events (Innovation Summit West, etc.). Smaller but more curated than JPM.

**IndieBio** — bio-leaning accelerator with strong investor network on biotech side. Has done medtech devices.

**MATTER (Chicago)** — healthcare innovation hub with curated investor connections.

### 4. Direct outreach (free, slowest, lowest hit rate)

LinkedIn and cold email. Covered in Session 001 outreach materials. Volume small and targeted (2-3 contacts per firm, staggered sends), not mass blast.

### 5. Strategic introductions via existing portco boards

Each portco board has 3-7 members. Each board member has a network of 50-200 active contacts. The arithmetic: 7 portcos × 5 board members × 50 networkable contacts = ~1,750 potential intros. At 1% conversion, 17 warm intros.

**Mechanism:** quarterly request to each portco board asking, "Who in your network is a fit for [next stage] capital? Would you make an intro?"

## External Help: Specific Options

### Tier 1: Highest-leverage hires (do one of these in the next 60 days)

**A. Fractional Head of Capital Strategy**
- 1-2 days/week, $8K-$15K/month
- Profile: ex-VP at Leerink/HC Wainwright/Piper Sandler healthcare practice, OR ex-Partner at medtech VC
- Where to find: LinkedIn search "ex-Leerink advisor", "former Versant partner consultant", "ex-Polaris healthcare". Healthcare executive search firms (Heidrick & Struggles, Spencer Stuart, Russell Reynolds healthcare practice) can source candidates.
- Vetting: reference-check with 3+ founders they've worked with. Ask for evidence of warm intros they've made in last 12 months.
- Value: 100+ warm investor relationships unlocked

**B. Ex-strategic-exec advisors (formal advisory board, equity)**
- 0.25%-0.5% equity per advisor over 2-4 year vest
- 2-3 from Medtronic, J&J, Stryker, Boston Scientific
- Bonus: add 1 ex-medtech VC partner (intros to VC side) + 1 ex-healthcare banker (intros to IB side)
- Value: strategic credibility, cross-network introductions
- Where to find: LinkedIn, healthcare exec network referrals, MedTech Strategist contributor list

### Tier 2: Accelerated network access (mid-cost, moderate value)

**C. MedTech Innovator engagement**
- Apply portcos to current accelerator cohort, OR
- Engage as graduate-company / studio partner if eligible
- Cost: portco time commitment, free or low cost for studio
- Value: 500+ company alumni network + 100+ investor demo day

**D. AdvaMed membership and event participation**
- Studio-level membership
- Cost: ~$5K-$15K/year by tier
- Value: industry positioning, investor introductions at events

**E. JPMorgan Healthcare Conference (already in plan)**
- Side dinners, hosted-meeting weeks (some firms pay to be on "track")
- Cost: $0-$50K depending on involvement level
- Value: 15-20 strategic meetings in 4 days

### Tier 3: Direct outreach engine (low cost, lower hit rate)

**F. Fundraising-focused VA / contractor**
- For LinkedIn outreach, CRM management, scheduling
- Cost: $1K-$3K/month
- Value: capacity multiplier for personal outreach time
- Risk: cold-email automation backfires if generic. Best used to support, not replace, personal outreach.

### Tier 4: Engage when stage-ready

**G. Outcome Capital retainer**
- Engage when first portco approaches Series A readiness
- Cost: $5K-$25K retainer + 5-7% success fee
- Value: full deal-process partnership, their entire client and investor book opens

## Recommended 6-Month Build Sequence

### Month 1-2: Internal mapping + key hire

1. Build `portco-investor-map.md` — every existing investor in every portco. Likely surfaces 30-60 names.
2. LinkedIn-search and interview 3-5 candidates for fractional Head of Capital Strategy. Hire one.
3. Initial outreach to 5-10 ex-strategic-exec advisory board candidates.
4. Edgemont → Revelation warm intro request (existing open item).

### Month 3-4: Network expansion via paid help

1. Fractional advisor introduces 10-15 named investor relationships per month.
2. Apply 1-2 portcos to MedTech Innovator current cohort.
3. Advisory board members start writing intro emails to their strategic networks.
4. Outcome Capital relationship moves from cold to active (Session 001 outreach finally lands).

### Month 5-6: Event preparation and direct outreach

1. JPM 2027 meeting list built. Target 20+ scheduled meetings.
2. AdvaMed membership active. First event attended.
3. Direct LinkedIn outreach to remaining unmet target investors.
4. Quarterly portfolio update launched to entire built network.

**End-state at 6 months:** 50+ named investor relationships across stages, 5+ secondary buyer relationships, 3+ ex-strategic execs on advisory board, 1 active fractional advisor, JPM 2027 meeting calendar full.
