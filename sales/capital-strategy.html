Track 1a · Pre-Seed and Seed

Grants, angels, and early-stage VCs.

Capital sources

  • Non-dilutive grants · SBIR Phase I/II, ARPA-H, BARDA, DoD (CDMRP, SOCOM, AFWERX), NIH
  • Foundation grants · Gates, Wellcome, RWJF, Helmsley for specific indications
  • Pre-seed / seed medtech VCs · BioInnovation Capital, LifeForce, KCK Group, DCVC Bio, F-Prime, Lux Capital
  • Hardware-friendly seed funds · Bolt VC, Hardware Club, 8VC, Day One
  • Strategic CVCs that go early · J&J Innovation / JJDC (only big-eight that writes seed checks), Olympus Innovation
  • Angel networks · sector-specific angels (ex-Medtronic / J&J / Stryker execs)
  • Accelerators · MedTech Innovator, IndieBio, MATTER

Approach

  • Grants first. Apply in parallel with equity. SBIR Phase I ($150-$300K) plus ARPA-H exploratory extends runway 12-18 months non-dilutively.
  • Studio network leverage. Build a named angel list of 15-25 sector-aligned investors. Quarterly update cadence.
  • Targeted VC intros. Pre-seed / seed medtech VCs read each other. One warm intro opens 3-5 doors.
  • Studio as anchor. 10XBeta's check signals confidence to external pre-seed investors.
  • NOT placement agents. Don't burn relationships on firms that can't help at this stage.

Recommended studio capability: a fractional grants writer. Even one SBIR Phase I award ($150-$300K) pays for years of this role.

Medtech VCs at Series A+

  • HealthQuest, Polaris Healthcare, F-Prime, Versant, Sofinnova, Catalyst Health

  • Targeted LinkedIn at VP / Director, Medical Devices

  • Contact-form fallback with corrected positioning
  • 2 to 3 contacts per firm, staggered sends

2 to 3 active placement-agent relationships. At least one with an active portfolio company mandate when a Series A becomes live.

Track 1b · Secondaries

Two lanes. Use both depending on the position.

Lane 1 · Incubated Companies

Standalone Secondaries

10XBeta majority equity. Large positions, deep unrealized gains, can absorb 10-20% discount in exchange for liquidity certainty and timing flexibility.

Buyers: Revelation Partners (priority via Edgemont intro), Industry Ventures, NewView Capital.

Lane 2 · Minority Positions

Integrated Tender

Smaller stakes, later positions. Pricing tied to round valuation, no discount. Capped percentage of round (10-20%). Best at Series B+ with traction.

Mechanism: Negotiate into primary round terms with lead investor approval.

The discount cost on Lane 1 is the price of timing certainty and recycling capital into new ventures. Lane 2 protects primary-investor relationships at younger stages.

Track 3 · 10XBeta Fund I

The fund earns the right to exist.

Why a fund eventually

At scale, the studio needs more capital than its balance sheet can deploy across portcos. A fund unlocks institutional LP capital that follows portcos through Series B, C, and beyond.

Why not now

Institutional LPs require signal: DPI, named exits, or a recognized anchor commitment. Raising too early forces a punishing cost of capital and stalls operating momentum.

Target LP types when activated

  • University endowments
  • Healthcare-focused fund-of-funds
  • Foundations (mission alignment)
  • Sovereign wealth in select cases
  • Strategic LP commitments from CVCs
  • Large family offices (anchor checks)

Risks & Tradeoffs

The things that could break this.

Strategic risks

  • Premature shopping signal. Talking to strategics too early can hurt primary fundraising for portcos.
  • Studio raise dilution. Priced round dilutes founders' studio equity. Term-sheet quality matters more than headline valuation.
  • Fund timing miss. Raising Fund I before signal forces high cost of capital and stalled operating momentum.

Execution risks

  • Stage-channel mismatch. Wrong channel for portco stage wastes relationship capital. Stage-by-stage discipline matters.
  • Capacity dilution. Three workstreams across multiple portco stages require disciplined sequencing or none get done well.
  • Domain credibility gap. Studio needs ex-Medtronic, ex-J&J, ex-Stryker advisors to translate the pitch to corporate dev.

Mitigation: stage-by-stage playbook discipline, advisory board build in Q4 2026, signal-first discipline on Fund I, confidence framing in every conversation.
