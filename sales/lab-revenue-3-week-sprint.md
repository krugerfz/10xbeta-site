## Operating mode for next 3 weeks

Frederick is in single-threaded lab revenue mode. **Capital strategy work drops to maintenance** for these 3 weeks:

- Monday capital review continues (30 min, RedZone + Validose status)
- Scheduled investor calls happen as planned
- New investor outreach paused
- Everything else is lab revenue outreach

This is a real tradeoff. RedZone and Validose seed close timelines will likely extend by ~3 weeks. Worth it if the lab close lands; existential if it doesn't.

### Daily activity targets

| Week | Outreach contacts/day | Goal by end of week |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | 5-10 warm contacts | 25-50 conversations initiated, 5-10 qualified opportunities |
| Week 2 | 5-7 contacts + 5-8 follow-ups | 3-5 proposals out, 1+ moving to close |
| Week 3 | Close mode | First signed engagement |

### Conversion expectations

- Warm contact with active need: ~25-40% convert to scoping conversation
- Scoping conversation: ~30-50% convert to proposal
- Proposal: ~30-50% convert to close
- Net: 30-50 warm conversations gives a realistic shot at 1-3 closes

Cold outreach won't move fast enough. Warm pool is the lever.

## Sprint offerings (productized fast-close engagements)

Scoped to fit individual signature authority (no committee approval). Designed for 2-4 week delivery. Outcomes are concrete and standalone-useful.

### Sprint 1: Feasibility Assessment

- **Price:** $25K
- **Duration:** 2 weeks
- **Scope:** Engineering feasibility review of a concept. Bill of materials and rough costing. Critical technical risks identified. Recommended development path.
- **Deliverable:** 10-15 page feasibility memo
- **Best for:** Early-stage spinouts, VC-portfolio companies still evaluating direction, corp innovation teams testing adjacencies
- **Signature authority:** Founder / CEO at startups, principal at VC firms, innovation officer at corps

### Sprint 2: Regulatory Pathway Analysis

- **Price:** $30K-$40K
- **Duration:** 2-3 weeks
- **Scope:** 510(k) / De Novo / PMA pathway assessment. Predicate device research. Timeline and cost estimate. Pre-sub meeting prep.
- **Deliverable:** Regulatory strategy memo + pre-sub package outline
- **Best for:** Spinouts approaching first FDA conversation, portcos planning regulatory path, corp teams testing a regulated adjacency
- **Signature authority:** CEO / CTO at startups, regulatory affairs head at corps

### Sprint 3: IP Landscape + Provisional Patent

- **Price:** $25K-$35K
- **Duration:** 2-3 weeks
- **Scope:** Patent landscape search. Freedom-to-operate preliminary assessment. Provisional patent drafted ready for outside counsel review/filing.
- **Deliverable:** IP strategy memo + provisional draft
- **Best for:** Pre-Series A spinouts that need IP clarity, projects ready to file before publication or disclosure event
- **Signature authority:** CEO / Chief Scientific Officer at startups, IP lead at corps

### Sprint 4: 4-Week POC Sprint

- **Price:** $50K-$100K
- **Duration:** 4 weeks
- **Scope:** Build a hardware or software POC of a specific feature. Bench testing.
- **Deliverable:** Working POC + technical report
- **Best for:** Concepts ready for proof-of-concept, "show me it works" milestone before next round
- **Signature authority:** CEO/CTO at startups, R&D director at corps

### Sprint 5: Hardware Design Sprint

- **Price:** $75K-$150K
- **Duration:** 6-8 weeks
- **Scope:** Mechanical or electrical design work. CAD files, schematics, prototype build.
- **Deliverable:** Functional prototype
- **Best for:** Concepts past feasibility, ready for first prototype
- **Signature authority:** May require board approval at higher amounts

### Anchoring strategy

Lead with Sprints 1-3 ($25K-$40K). These fit single-signoff authority and have the fastest decision cycles. Sprints 4-5 are stretch and useful as upsells once trust is built.

These are categories most likely to convert in 3 weeks. Frederick has the actual names; categories prioritize where to look:

4. **Studio's collective network** — CEOs, CTOs, COOs in medtech and adjacent in the broader 10XBeta network. Past collaborations, deal partners, advisory relationships across the team.
5. **Defense contacts from RedZone work** — ARPA-H, SOCOM program officers, defense-tech alumni at VC funds. These may have civilian / dual-use needs separate from RedZone.
6. **Universities with prior relationships** — anyone who has hosted us, where we've spoken, where we have advisory relationships.
7. **Past 10XBeta candidates and interviewees** — engineers who interviewed but didn't join often work at companies needing exactly this kind of dev work.

For each category, the question is: "Who do I know in this group who has expressed even mild interest in lab capacity, or who might have a current need?"

## What to skip during the 3-week sprint

- **Long pitch decks** — the outreach template + 30-minute call is the entire selling motion. Decks come after "yes."
- **Lab tours** — don't offer unless absolutely needed. Half-day investment. Only for $50K+ scope or strategic-anchor relationship.
- **MSA negotiation** — use a simple 2-page SOW for sprint engagements. Defer MSA to ongoing relationships.
- **Full case study packages** — single-paragraph case study reference in conversation works fine. Spiro Wave, RedZone, one drug-delivery example.
- **Multi-stakeholder approval processes** — target single-signoff scope. If a deal needs three approvals, it won't close in 3 weeks.
- **Custom pricing for each conversation** — use the published sprint prices. Negotiate only at close, not in scoping.

## Decision gates by week

### End of Week 1

- &check; 25-50 outreach conversations initiated
- &check; 5-10 qualified opportunities in pipeline (replied with interest, identified a real need)
- &check; 1-3 scoping conversations completed
- &cross; **If pipeline is &lt;5:** pivot to broader cold outreach to named targets in main strategy doc, OR activate broader network harder, OR lower the sprint scope (drop to $15K rapid-assessment offerings)

### End of Week 2

- &check; 3-5 proposals out (SOW + price sent)
- &check; At least 1 client verbally committed and paperwork moving
- &cross; **If no proposals out:** sprint scope is wrong (too large for fast signoff?) or pricing is wrong (too high?). Adjust both immediately.

### End of Week 3

- &check; First signed engagement, work scheduled
- &cross; **If nothing signed:** assess what blocked. Was scope too ambitious? Did approval cycles lock the deal? Was sprint pricing the wrong anchor? Lower threshold for Week 4 (drop entry sprint to $10-15K rapid assessment, or offer initial 1-week sprint as paid pilot).

## After first close

Once first engagement is signed, the 3-week sprint mode ends. Subsequent work follows the main `sales/lab-revenue-strategy.md` strategy:

- Continue Sprint 1-3 offerings as the entry product across all channels

- Build curve targets: months 5-6 = $50-150K/month, months 7-9 = $150-300K/month, etc.
- Trigger point for BD hire reassessed once $1M+ in qualified pipeline is real
