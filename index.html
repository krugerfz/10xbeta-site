The challenge

The gap between idea and made is where most things die.

Getting from zero to one means crossing the trap between a concept that works on paper and a product that works in the real world. Most teams underestimate four things.

01

The pathway

The documentation, testing, and approvals a regulated product needs are the bulk of the work, not an afterthought.

02

The market

Demand has to be proven before launch, not assumed after it. Real commitments beat enthusiasm.

03

The build

Hardware, software, and AI decisions made in isolation create rework. They have to be designed together.

04

The handoff

Manufacturing decisions made late add months and lock in the wrong cost. They belong at the start.

Manufacture · The Factory

The problem

Getting to market is a manufacturing trap.

You need hundreds of units. The system is built for hundreds of thousands, and it asks you to pay for expensive tooling before you've sold a single one.

How we're different

We own the process.
Not the machines.

We match the manufacturing process to the volume you actually need, and orchestrate the partner who runs it.
A factory owns machines, so it sells you the volume its machines are built for. We are not a factory. We select the right process (bridge tooling, cast urethane, CNC, 3D-printed production, or hard tooling) and run it through the partner that fits. Asset-light for us. Risk-light for you.
Where we fit
